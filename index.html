Petalhug
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-27

Summary

RiverMountain Studio ("we") makes the mobile app Petalhug ("the app"). We do not collect any personal data. The app has no account and no server, and everything you enter (tasks, records, moods, room) stays on your phone. The app uses the internet only for ads (when you open the app, the ad library checks consent and gets an ad ready; ads are shown only when you choose to watch one) and to check Google Play purchases.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your to-do list, including tasks you type yourself, and which ones you checked each day
  • The mood sticker you picked each day
  • Materials, decorations and how your room is arranged
  • Settings: language, reminder on/off and reminder time
  • Simple counts of the days you opened the app (on your own or from the reminder), shown to you on the Records screen

To protect your records, the app keeps two copies of your save and a daily backup of the last 3 days, all inside the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive this data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone settings, deletes it. The app's Android cloud backup is currently turned off, so this data is not copied to your Google account.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications — only if you allow it. Used for one reminder a day at the time you choose. You can turn it off in the app (Records screen) or in your phone settings, and the app works fully without it.
  • Run at startup — to set the daily reminder again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration — for small feedback when you tap.
  • Internet and network state — to load ads and to check Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID — for Google AdMob ads (see "Ads"). You can reset or delete it in your phone settings.
  • Google Play billing — to buy decoration bundles and restore purchases.
  • Other normal permissions (added by the ad and billing libraries, not asked at install) — mobile network type, keeping background tasks running.

The app does not ask for contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data, because we do not receive any. Ads are handled directly by Google (AdMob) and payments by Google Play (see "Ads" and "In-app purchases").

Ads

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. When you open the app, the ad library contacts Google's servers to check consent and get an ad ready. At that time and when you watch an ad, Google (Google LLC, USA) may process your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and records of ads viewed or tapped, to show, measure and analyse ads and to prevent fraud. This data is sent to Google's servers in the USA and other countries, and Google's policies decide how long it is kept. We do not receive this data. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch them (for example, to get one more material); not watching never takes anything away. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads, and can change it at any time in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. To restore purchases, the app asks Google Play which items you own.

Cloud backup

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up the app's save to your Google account so you can restore it on a new phone. This is handled by Google under your Google account settings; we cannot access it.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because your data stays on your phone, you are in control of it: you can edit your records in the app, and delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them 1 year after the matter is closed.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently (for example, by adding ads or purchases), we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
